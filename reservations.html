Reservation Guidelines

  • Reservations can be made up to 30 days in advance
  • Please arrive 10 minutes before your reservation time
  • For parties larger than 8, please call us directly
  • Your table will be held for 15 minutes after your reservation time
  • Special requests are accommodated based on availability

  • Friday - Saturday: 8:00 AM - 11:00 PM

Last reservation accepted 1 hour before closing

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee for reservations?

No, reservations are completely free of charge. However, for no-shows or cancellations less than 2 hours before the reservation time, we reserve the right to note this in our system.

Can I request a specific table?

You can mention your preference in the special requests field, and we'll do our best to accommodate based on availability, but specific tables cannot be guaranteed.

How long can we stay at our table?

For parties of 2-4, tables are reserved for up to 2 hours. For larger parties, tables are reserved for up to 2.5 hours. If you need more time, please let us know in advance.

Do you have high chairs for children?

Yes, we have high chairs and booster seats available. Please mention this in your special requests when making a reservation.
